Story SHIP-9: Shipping fee at checkout

As a shopper, I want to see the right shipping fee at checkout, so that I know what I will pay.

- AC-1: Standard shipping is free when the order subtotal is $50.00 or more; otherwise it costs $5.99.
- AC-2: Members get free standard shipping at any subtotal.
- AC-3: Express shipping costs $12.99, except for members, who pay $7.99 for express at any subtotal.
