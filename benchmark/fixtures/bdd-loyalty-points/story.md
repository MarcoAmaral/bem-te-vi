Story LOY-3: Loyalty points

As a member of the loyalty programme, I want to earn and redeem points, so that I get discounts on later orders.

- AC-1: A member earns 1 point for every whole dollar of an order's subtotal ($24.99 earns 24 points).
- AC-2: A member's tier is Basic below 500 points, Silver from 500 points and Gold from 2,000 points. Silver members get 5% off every order and Gold members 10%.
- AC-3: Redeeming more points than the balance shows "You don't have enough points." and redeems nothing.
- AC-4: The points page should load fast.
