Feature: Loyalty points
  As a member of the loyalty programme
  I want to earn and redeem points
  So that I get discounts on later orders

  @regression @p1 @loyalty
  Scenario: Points are earned per whole dollar
    Given a member with 0 points
    When they place an order with a subtotal of $24.99
    Then their balance is 24 points

  @regression @p1 @loyalty
  Scenario Outline: Tier follows the balance
    Given a member with <points> points
    Then their tier is <tier>

    Examples:
      | points | tier   |
      | 499    | Basic  |
      | 500    | Silver |
      | 1999   | Silver |
      | 2000   | Gold   |

  @regression @p1 @loyalty
  Scenario: Redeeming more than the balance is refused
    Given a member with 100 points
    When they redeem 101 points
    Then they see "You don't have enough points."
