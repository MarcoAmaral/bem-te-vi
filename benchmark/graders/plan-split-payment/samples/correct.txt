# Test Plan — Split payment

## 1. Context of testing
Scope: paying one order with two cards through PayGate.

## 2. Assumptions and constraints
The sandbox is available on Tuesdays and Thursdays only.

## 3. Stakeholders
Product owner, two developers, one tester at half time.

## 4. Communication
Daily note in the team channel.

## 5. Risk register

| # | ID | Risk | Category | L | I | Score | Level |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | R01 | Remainder cent put on the wrong card (rounding) | Product | 3 | 4 | 12 | HIGH |
| 2 | R02 | First charge not refunded within 60 s after a decline | Product | 3 | 5 | 15 | CRITICAL |
| 3 | R03 | Sandbox available only two days a week delays testing | Project | 4 | 3 | 12 | HIGH |
| 4 | R04 | Single-card checkout regresses | Product | 2 | 5 | 10 | HIGH |

## 6. Test approach
Entry criteria: feature deployed to staging. Exit criteria: every release gate case passes.
Release gate: R02 refund, R01 rounding, R04 single-card regression.

## 7. Estimate
Six tester days, range five to eight.

## 8. Budget and schedule
Within the 10 working days.

## 9. Configuration management of testware
Cases and data in the repository.
